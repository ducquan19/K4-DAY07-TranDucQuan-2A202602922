# VinUni Undergraduate Tuition Fee Structure and Credit-Based Rules

VinUniversity believes that every talented student deserves access to world-class education, regardless of their financial background. To support admitted students and their families, VinUniversity provides a founder-funded tuition subsidy alongside comprehensive merit scholarships and need-based financial aid.

## 1. Listed Tuition Fees (Academic Year 2026–2027)

Tuition fees are structured on an annual, semester, and per-credit basis:

- **Bachelor of Nursing (4 years)**:
  - Annual Listed Fee: 349,650,000 VND
  - Semester Fee: 174,825,000 VND
  - Per-Credit Listed Fee: 9,780,000 VND
- **Doctor of Medicine (MD, 6 years)**:
  - Annual Listed Fee: 815,850,000 VND
  - Semester Fee: 407,925,000 VND
  - Per-Credit Listed Fee: 27,195,000 VND
- **Other Bachelor Programs (Business Administration, Engineering, Computer Science - 4 years)**:
  - Annual Listed Fee: 815,850,000 VND
  - Semester Fee: 407,925,000 VND
  - Per-Credit Listed Fee: 27,195,000 VND

## 2. 35% Founder-Funded Tuition Subsidy

All admitted students (both domestic Vietnamese and international students) automatically receive a **35% tuition subsidy** directly funded by Vingroup, the Founder of VinUniversity.
- This 35% subsidy is maintained throughout the student's entire standard duration of study.
- The listed tuition fee is an all-inclusive comprehensive fee covering: academic teaching, clinical/laboratory practice, campus facility usage, IT infrastructure/software licenses, and university student activities (orientation, health services, psychological counseling, academic advising, career guidance, and student clubs).

## 3. Circumstances where Credit-Based Tuition is Charged

The listed per-credit fee (9,780,000 VND for Nursing; 27,195,000 VND for MD and other majors) is specifically charged in the following scenarios:
1. **Double Degrees**: Enrolling in supplementary credits to complete two separate degree programs simultaneously at VinUniversity.
2. **Credits Beyond Standard Curriculum**: Credits taken in excess of the standard academic program (1 major + up to 1 minor), such as dual concentrations or double minors.
3. **Integrated Exchange Programs**: Taking supplementary exchange credits at designated partner universities abroad.
4. **Course Retakes and Grade Improvement**: Repeating a course due to failure (grade below minimum requirement), ineligibility for final exam due to attendance shortage or academic misconduct, or voluntary retake to improve cumulative GPA.
5. **Credit Transfer with Conditional Requirements**: Admitted transfer students who require supplementary practical or laboratory credits to fulfill degree equivalence.
